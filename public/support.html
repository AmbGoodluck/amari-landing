Getting started

  • Amari works in your browser at app.askamari.us, on your phone or your computer. There's nothing to download. The iPhone app is in testing.
  • Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome, open the menu, then tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • No account needed to start. Answer a few quick questions, then snap your fridge, or try the sample fridge to see how it works first.
  • Amari is free for everyone right now. See Free and fair use below.

Scanning your fridge or cupboard

  • Take one or more photos of your fridge or cupboard, and Amari lists the food it sees. Good light and an open, uncluttered shelf give the best results.
  • Review the list. Confirm, fix or remove anything Amari wasn't sure about, and add what it missed.
  • Add food by hand at any time: search for an ingredient and add it, no photo needed.
  • Before your first scan, Amari asks your permission to send photos to an AI service. If you'd rather not, you can add everything by hand.
  • Your photos are never stored. Only the names of the foods found are kept.
  • Please photograph food only, not people, documents or anything private.

Allergies, diets and food safety

  • Set your allergies, diets and foods you avoid once, and every suggestion respects them. Amari covers the 9 major allergens (milk or dairy, egg, peanut, tree nuts, wheat, soy, fish, shellfish and sesame), 7 diets (vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, gluten-free, halal, no pork and no beef) and an avoid list for anything else.
  • Every ingredient in every recipe is checked against your rules, and Ask Amari's answers are checked too, with a warning if anything conflicts.
  • Amari is a guide, not a guarantee. Recipes and products vary. Always read product labels, especially for allergens and cross-contamination warnings, and don't rely on Amari alone if you have a severe allergy.
  • Nutrition figures are estimates. Amari doesn't give medical advice.
  • In an emergency, such as a severe allergic reaction, call your local emergency number. See section 6 of the Terms of Use.

Accounts and syncing across devices

  • You can use Amari without an account. Create one only when you want your kitchen and plans on another device.
  • Sign in with Apple, Google or email (a one-time code or a password).
  • When you're signed in, your saved recipes, ratings, goals, allergies, diets, preferences, kitchen, weekly plan and shopping list are kept with your account and appear on every device where you sign in.
  • Amari works offline for your kitchen, recipes, plans and list, and syncs again when you're back online.
  • Something missing on another device? Check that you're signed in with the same account on both, and open Amari on both while you're online.
  • Signing out clears Amari from that device. Your information stays in your account for the next time you sign in.

Reminders and timers

  • Reminders are off until you turn them on in the app and allow notifications when your device asks.
  • You can choose dinner-time reminders on planned nights, a shopping reminder the evening before your plan starts, use-soon alerts for food about to go off, and cooking-timer alerts.
  • Set quiet hours. Amari sends at most one reminder a day.
  • Notifications never mention your weight, goals, allergies or calories.
  • News from Amari (new recipes and features) is a separate choice, off unless you turn it on.

Ask Amari

  • Ask cooking questions about your own kitchen, like "What can I make in 20 minutes?" or "What can I use instead of buttermilk?"
  • Ask Amari knows what's in your kitchen, your allergies, diet, tastes, time and household size, so its answers fit your night.
  • If the collection doesn't have the dish you want, Ask Amari can write a new recipe for you.
  • Amari asks your permission before it first sends your information to an AI service.
  • Your conversations aren't stored. AI can be wrong, so use your judgement and check ingredients yourself.

Free and fair use

  • Every feature, including Ask Amari, is free for everyone right now. No payment details are needed.
  • Features have fair-use limits that keep Amari free, such as a number of scans per day, Ask Amari messages per month and new recipes per month. The app tells you when you reach a limit, and limits reset automatically.
  • If paid plans are ever introduced, you'll be told in the app first, and nothing is charged unless you choose to buy it.

Your data and privacy

  • Download your data: in the app, go to You → Privacy and data.
  • Delete your account and everything stored with it: You → Privacy and data → Delete account.
  • Analytics are off unless you turn them on, and you can turn crash reports off, in You → Privacy and data.
  • For any other privacy request, email support@askamari.us from the address linked to your account. We respond within 30 days.
  • Read the full Privacy Policy.

Something not working?

  • Check your internet connection, then close and reopen Amari, or reload the page in your browser.
  • Improvements reach the app automatically, so reopening it usually brings the latest version.
  • Send feedback from inside the app: go to You → Send feedback, or use the feedback button on any recipe. If you've turned notifications on, replies come back as a notification.
  • Or email support@askamari.us with the device and browser you're using, what you were doing, what happened, and a screenshot if you can.
  • Never send your password or sign-in code to anyone, including us.
