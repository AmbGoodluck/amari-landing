Contact

We read every message.

The fastest way to reach the Amari team is by email.

Support and questions

Email support@askamari.us about anything: help using Amari, a recipe that didn't work, an idea or a problem. Include the device and browser you're using if something isn't working.

Feedback from inside the app

Use You → Send feedback, or the feedback button on any recipe. Replies come back to you as a notification if you've turned notifications on.

Privacy requests

You can download your data or delete your account at any time in the app, at You → Privacy and data. For any other privacy request, email support@askamari.us from the address linked to your account. Section 14 of the Privacy Policy explains your rights.

Food safety

Amari is a guide, not a guarantee. If you have a severe allergy, always read product labels yourself. In an emergency, such as a severe allergic reaction, call your local emergency number. See section 6 of the Terms of Use.
