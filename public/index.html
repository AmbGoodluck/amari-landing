Ask Amari

Your AI cook, who knows your kitchen.

Ask Amari knows what’s in your fridge, your allergies, your tastes, how much time you have and how many you’re feeding, so its answers fit your night, not a generic one.

  • “What can I make in 20 minutes?” “What can I use instead of buttermilk?” “Use up my spinach.”
  • Writes a new recipe, with a photo, when the collection doesn’t have the dish you want.
  • Every answer is checked against your allergies and diets, with a warning if anything conflicts.
  • Remembers your dislikes and adds missing items to your shopping list.

Your rules, always

Checked against every ingredient, every time.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? Email support@askamari.us.

What is Amari?

Amari is an AI kitchen assistant. It starts with what’s already in your fridge and cupboard, shows the meals you can cook tonight, and helps you plan, shop and cook step by step. It’s available as a web app you can install to your home screen; the iPhone app is in testing.

How does ingredient scanning work?

Take one or more photos of your fridge or cupboard. The photos are resized on your device, sent to an AI model that lists the food it recognises, and then discarded. You review the list, fix anything it got wrong and add what it missed. Scan as often as you shop; fair-use limits keep Amari free.

Does Amari work with pantry items?

Yes. Scan your cupboard as well as your fridge, or add pantry staples by hand. Everything is organised by category in your kitchen, with use-soon reminders for food that won’t last.

Can I specify dietary preferences and allergies?

Yes. Amari covers the 9 major allergens (milk, egg, peanut, tree nuts, wheat, soy, fish, shellfish and sesame), 7 diets (vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, gluten-free, halal, no pork and no beef) and an avoid list for anything else. Recipes are checked against your rules by fixed logic, and Ask Amari’s answers are checked too. It’s a guide, not a guarantee: always read product labels, especially if you have a severe allergy.

Can I add ingredients manually?

Yes. Search for any ingredient and add it without taking a photo. You can also edit or remove anything after a scan.

Does Amari generate recipes?

Amari suggests dishes from a collection of 435 recipes, each checked for ingredients, steps and safety before it’s added. If the collection doesn’t have the dish you want, Ask Amari can write a new recipe for you with AI. AI can be wrong, so use your judgement and check ingredients yourself.

Is Amari free?

Yes. Every feature, including Ask Amari, calories and macros, and weekly autopilot, is free for everyone right now, with fair-use limits. If paid plans are ever introduced, you’ll be told in the app first, and nothing is charged unless you choose to buy it.

Is my information secure?

Your kitchen, plans and preferences live on your device first. Fridge photos are never stored, information is encrypted in transit and at rest, there are no ads and no data selling, and you can delete your account in one tap. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.

Is there an iPhone app?

The iPhone app is in testing and isn’t available yet. You can use Amari today in your browser at app.askamari.us and install it to your home screen.
